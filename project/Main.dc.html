About

I'm a freelance photographer. I shoot weddings and portraits, and I care most about the moments people don't notice themselves having: a laugh halfway through a toast, a hand straightened on a collar.

I work quietly and keep direction light, so you can spend your day being with the people you love. You get photographs that look like you, not like a pose.
